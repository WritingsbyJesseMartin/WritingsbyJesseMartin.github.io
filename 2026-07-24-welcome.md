## How this works

Every file in the `_posts` folder becomes one blog entry. The filename controls the date and URL — the format is always:

```
YYYY-MM-DD-your-title-here.md
```

The newest post automatically appears larger at the top of the homepage. Everything else lists below it, newest to oldest.
